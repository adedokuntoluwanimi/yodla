# Starter product image sources

These product images are included for Yodla's authorised starter catalogue. Before any broader catalogue release, replace or supplement them with the final brand/distributor image pack and retain the relevant usage documentation.

## Supplied brand photography

- `bottega-gold` — Bottega Gold Prosecco product image.
- `hennessy-vs` — Hennessy VS product image.
- `jack-daniels` — Jack Daniel's Old No. 7 product image.
- `heineken-original` — Heineken Original Lager product image.

## Generated Yodla concepts

Fictional Yodla Studio concepts live under `assets/products/originals/` (for example `indigo-vodka`, `palm-botanical-gin`, `copper-palm-rum`, `midnight-red`, `lagos-sunset-rose`, `ginger-lime`, `malt-reserve`, and the hero set images). Keep them labelled as concepts in catalogue data and on product detail pages.

## Responsive derivatives

Compact and large WebP (and hero AVIF) variants are generated into `assets/products/responsive/` by `npm run assets`. Do not hand-edit those files; regenerate from originals when source art changes.
